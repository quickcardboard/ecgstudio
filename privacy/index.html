ECG Studio privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · applies to the website ecgstudio.app and the ECG Studio Android app

In short

ECG Studio does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your Training progress (ratings, history, reviews and streak) and a few display settings are saved only in your browser or in the app on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • You can delete them at any time: Training's menu → Reset progress, clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app.
  • Every ECG is generated on your device; nothing you type or answer leaves it.

When you choose to share

Sharing a scenario, an ECG image, a daily result or a problem report uses your phone's own share sheet or clipboard. It goes only where you send it (for example an email you write to us), and is handled by the app you choose.

Purchases

Purchases in the Android app are made through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we receive no payment details and only learn, on your device, whether a purchase is active.

Services that see technical data

  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any website, these services receive your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages; see the GitHub and Google privacy policies.
  • The Android app works offline and makes no network requests of its own. Google Play may collect data about installs and crashes under Google's policies.

Children

ECG Studio is made for students and health professionals and is not directed at children.

Medical use

ECG Studio is an educational simulator, not a medical device. It is not for diagnosing or treating patients.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: tlioudevelop@gmail.com.
