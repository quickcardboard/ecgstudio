Statistics

Help

Read the ECG and type what you see: the rhythm first, then every abnormality. Press Submit when you are done.

The ECG

  • Swipe left or right on the ECG to scroll.
  • Double-tap for the full 12-lead sideways. Double-tap again or ✕ to come back.
  • Paper at 25 mm/s and 10 mm/mV. Rate, PR, QRS, QTc and axis are measured for you above the ECG.

Answering

  • Suggestions appear after 2 letters, most common first. Abbreviations work.
  • Findings that need a place (Q waves, ST elevation, ST depression, T inversion) ask you to tap the lead groups on the ECG.
  • Tap a chip to remove it.

Scoring

  • The rhythm is 40% of the score; the findings share the other 60% (the location is half of a finding). When the rhythm is the only abnormality, it is the whole score.
  • True but secondary findings earn a small bonus; findings that are not there cost 10%.
  • Advanced findings (Wellens pattern, the Osborn waves of hypothermia, a STEMI read through LBBB by the Sgarbossa criteria, hypo- and hypercalcemia) can appear at any level, but are asked only from a rating of 1700 in their skill. Before that, naming one earns the bonus and leaving it out costs nothing.
  • Reviews: ECGs you missed or got partly right come back after 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days.

Ratings

You have a rating for each skill: rhythm, conduction, ischemia and conditions. Each ECG has a difficulty from its rhythm and the number and type of its abnormalities. Progress is kept on this phone. Your rating will increase or decrease depending on your answers. Harder ECGs will move your rating higher, while missing on easy ECGs will move your rating lower.

Other

  • ⚑ Report a problem (under the result, or in the ⋯ menu) sends the author everything needed to check that ECG.
  • Model opens the simulator on the same heart, so you can see what produced the ECG.

About these ECGs

Every ECG is generated on your phone by the ECG Studio heart model. Before it is shown, the rhythm and P waves are checked from the heart’s own timing, and each abnormality is measured against its textbook criteria and must visibly change the tracing; only ECGs that pass are used.
