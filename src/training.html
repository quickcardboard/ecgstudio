How training works

Read the ECG and type what you see: the rhythm first, then every abnormality. Press Submit when you have named everything.

The ECG

  • The screen shows half of a printed 12-lead at a time: the limb leads (first 5 s) or the chest leads (last 5 s), each lead for 2.5 s, with the lead II rhythm strip below.
  • Swipe left or right for the other half.
  • Double-tap for the full 12-lead sideways, or turn the phone sideways. Double-tap again or ✕ to come back.
  • Paper at 25 mm/s and 10 mm/mV. Rate, PR, QRS, QTc and axis are measured for you above the ECG.

Answering

  • Suggestions appear after 2 letters, most common first. Abbreviations work: AF, CHB, 3AVB, LBBB, STE, TWI…
  • Findings that need a place (Q waves, ST elevation, ST depression, T inversion) ask you to tap the lead groups on the ECG: septal, anterior, lateral, inferior or aVR.
  • Tap a chip to remove it.

Scoring

  • The rhythm is 40% of the score; the findings share the other 60% (the location is half of a finding). When the rhythm is the only abnormality, it is the whole score.
  • ⚑ Report a problem (under the result, or in the ⋯ menu) sends the author everything needed to check that ECG.
  • True but secondary findings earn a small bonus; findings that are not there cost 10%.
  • Model opens the simulator on the same heart, so you can see what produced the ECG.

About these ECGs

Every ECG is generated on your phone by the ECG Studio heart model: a rhythm and up to four abnormalities chosen for your level. Before it is shown, an independent automated reader reads it blind; only ECGs whose reading matches the answer are used, so every abnormality is visible on the tracing.

Ratings

You have a rating for each skill: rhythm, conduction, ischemia and conditions. Each ECG has a difficulty from its rhythm and the number and type of its abnormalities, and the next ECG is chosen so that you get about three in four right. Progress is kept on this phone.
