# ECG Studio Training vocabulary (draft for the author to edit)

# One term per line:   id | Name shown on the chip | synonyms and abbreviations, comma-separated | flags
#   - "id" is what the answer keys use; do not rename an id that a question uses (tools/trainbank.js).
#   - Typing matches the start of any word of the name or a synonym ("fib" finds "Atrial fibrillation").
#   - flag "region": the finding needs a location; the ECG asks for the lead groups after it is picked.
# The section line (## Rhythms / ## Findings) decides whether a term answers the rhythm or a finding.
